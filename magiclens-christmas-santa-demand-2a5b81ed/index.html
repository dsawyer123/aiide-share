01 · The documented strategy

Relevance as a proxy for demand and conversion.  

Scope of Analysis: Cluster Map Output

The cluster map defines “Santa & Christmas” as the primary occasion and includes 48 phrases from a seed list.

Seed list examples include Santa videos, personalized Santa messages, Santa caught on camera, video messages from Santa, Christmas videos, and Christmas keepsakes. 

Qualification Method: Prioritizes Conversion

The qualification sequence is seed → expand → qualify → cluster → map → measure. Qualification explicitly uses intent, business fit and difficulty, with low-intent/high-difficulty terms disqualified due to high cost and low or no likelihood of converting.

Example Hero Phrase for Conversion (BOFU):
   🎅🏼 “personalized Santa video for kids” 🎥 

Caveat / Risk: This demand analysis selects the keywords with the highest probability of 'winning' and 'converting', but it does not prove that every selected phrase converts, nor that the probability of conversion is high.  Likewise, it does not prove that broad terms won't convert at a reasonable cost; however, it does accurately predict that 'unqualified' keywords will convert at a lower rate and higher cost than 'qualified' keywords.  

Evidence qualification: the relative conversion-rate and cost prediction above is a targeting hypothesis. The archive does not contain campaign conversion or acquisition-cost results proving that comparison.

The original cluster marked all 48 phrases as occasion-gift. The intent groups below refine that working classification: “free”, “calls” and watch-oriented searches.

04 · Christmas-season English-market proxy

Apply the multiplier to November–December demand.

Core market definition: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Ireland and New Zealand. The proxy estimates seasonal demand for each listed phrase in those markets; it is not a measured country total.

The working assumption: 1.5× US

The July analysis reports a 1.74× global/US ratio for its gift-and-occasion sample. Using 1.5× assumes most, but not all, of that non-US uplift is in six core English-speaking markets.

Applying that annual-sample relationship to Christmas is an additional assumption: geographic seasonality may differ. Use 1.3×–1.7× to test sensitivity, not as statistical confidence limits.

The seasonal calculation

(November US + December US) × multiplier. Only rows with both archived monthly values receive a seasonal proxy. Annual averages are never used to manufacture missing monthly values.

The archive has no global monthly series for these terms. Therefore there is no global seasonal cap; an annual-average global figure cannot cap a Christmas-season total.

05 · Campaign implications

Match each promise to the intent 

1 · Santa video

Show the personal experience

Lead with a finished sample and the available name/photo/message personalization. Make price, output format, delivery and creation steps easy to understand. Prioritize relevant personalized-video queries as a test shortlist.

2 · Image / keepsake

Give a reason to gift or keep it

Test a Christmas portrait, image or video keepsake proposition against relevant photo-gift and keepsake searches. Clearly explain whether the result is digital, printable or something else the product actually supports.

3 · Funnel

Optimize for completed outcomes

Compare landing visits → creation starts → successful outputs → purchases by keyword group and device. Evaluate acquisition cost against contribution after generation costs. Use engagement as supporting evidence.

What the evidence supports

A focused Christmas Momento conversion test with seasonal timing and differentiated landing pages. Personalized Santa video has direct relevance, measured US demand and a pronounced holiday peak. Gifts and keepsakes broaden the relevant discovery route but require a stronger explanation of product fit.

What still needs validation

Current bidding costs, live search-result intent, actual conversion, product readiness and a country-level demand split. Expansion should seek additional relevant phrases such as Santa video messages, personalized Christmas portraits or photo-to-Santa experiences—not volume for its own sake. “AI Santa video” can be tested for relevance; its absence is not evidence that the existing conversion strategy was wrong.
